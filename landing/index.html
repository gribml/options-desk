What it does today

Tracks what you own, lot by lot

Every purchase keeps its own cost basis and its own holding-period clock, so the app knows which of your shares have crossed the one-year line into the lower tax rate — and which haven't.

Models the trade before you place it

Name a price and a date, then lay out what you'd do. You get the cash it produces, the gain it realises, whether your shares get called away, and how much of your upside you've signed away by selling the call.

Puts a real number on the tax

Not a flat percentage. Progressive brackets, the preferential long-term rates, capital-loss netting and the net investment income tax, all computed against the income you actually expect this year — so you see the extra tax the trade itself causes.
